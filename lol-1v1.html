Start a LoL duel

Free beta. You start with 500 CLU, internal credits with no cash value. The first 50 players keep a permanent Founder badge.

How a duel works

  1. Sign in and link your game handle once.
  2. Create a challenge: pick League of Legends, the format, and how many CLU you are putting in.
  3. Send the link to a rival, or take one of the open challenges.
  4. Play. The result is verified, and the winner is credited automatically.

How the result is verified

We ask the Riot API for the match you just played, read it, and settle the duel on what it says. No screenshots, no trusting either player’s word.

Duel formats players use

Howling Abyss 1v1

The classic settle-it duel. First blood, 100 CS, or two kills, whoever gets there first.

Ranked solo performance

Same queue, separate games, compared on KDA, CS at 15, or damage share.

Custom lane matchup

Mid vs mid, top vs top. Agree the lane, play it, the data decides.

Questions

Is this gambling?

No. There is no house, no odds, and no random outcome. You win a duel by playing better, and during the free beta there is no money on the platform at all: everyone gets 500 CLU, which are internal credits that cannot be cashed out.

Which regions work?

Every Riot region resolves, and the beta is focused on Europe (EUW and EUNE) because that is where the first players are.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You link your Riot ID once, in the form Name#EUW, and play League exactly as you normally do.

What if the API is down when we finish?

The duel stays open until the match can be read. Nothing settles on a guess.

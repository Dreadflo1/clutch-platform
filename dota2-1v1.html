How a duel works

  1. Sign up and link your Steam64 ID once (the 7656119... number on your Steam profile).
  2. Create a challenge: pick Dota 2, the mid 1v1 format, and the CLU entry.
  3. Send the link to your rival, or take an open challenge from the board.
  4. Play the match. CLUTCH reads it from the Steam API and credits the winner.

How the result is verified

We look up the match you both played through the Steam Web API and settle on what it records: who drew first blood, who took two towers. No screenshots and no taking anyone at their word. Your Dota 2 match data needs to be public (Dota 2 settings, Social, Expose Public Match Data) so the match can be read.

Pick rules

Questions

Is this gambling?

No. There is no house, no odds and no random outcome. You win a duel by playing better, and during the free beta there is no money on the platform: everyone gets 500 CLU, internal credits that cannot be cashed out.

Why does my match data need to be public?

The Steam API only returns matches from players who expose their match data. Turn it on once in the Dota 2 settings and every duel can settle automatically.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You link your Steam64 ID once and play Dota 2 exactly as you normally do.

What if the API cannot read the match?

The duel stays open until the match can be read. Nothing settles on a guess.
